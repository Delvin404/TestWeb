Slurp Check wants to add your precise GPS location to this ticket. If you say yes, your browser will show its own permission prompt — nothing happens until you approve that too.

Advanced fingerprint (opt-in, heavier)

Everything on this ticket is read by standard browser JavaScript — the same APIs any site can call, nothing hacked or hidden. The deep scan adds canvas/audio/GPU fingerprinting and a WebRTC local-network probe — techniques real tracking scripts use to identify devices even without cookies. Modern browsers partly defend against these (Chrome masks your real local IP behind a random ".local" name, for instance), so results vary by browser. Precise GPS only appears if you approve the permission prompt. Nothing here is sent anywhere or saved; it disappears when you close the tab.
